Full Home Inspection in Arizona

The standard for any real estate transaction in Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, or anywhere in Arizona. Our full home inspection is a comprehensive, top-to-bottom evaluation of every visible and accessible system and component in the property - giving you the complete picture before you sign.

Our licensed Arizona home inspectors spend 2–4 hours on site examining over 200 checkpoints, documenting findings with photographs and clear, jargon-free descriptions. Whether you're buying your first home or your tenth investment property, you'll receive a report that's detailed enough to negotiate with and clear enough to actually understand.

Mold Testing & Air Quality Inspection in Arizona

Arizona's extreme summer heat combined with monsoon moisture creates ideal conditions for mold growth - especially in HVAC systems, bathrooms, under sinks, and in areas with any previous water damage. Many Arizona homeowners and buyers don't realize mold is a serious issue in the desert.

Our certified mold testing professionals collect air and surface samples, send them to an accredited third-party lab, and deliver a detailed report identifying mold types, spore counts, and recommended remediation steps if needed.

What's Included

  • Visual inspection of high-risk areas (HVAC, bathrooms, crawl spaces)
  • Indoor air quality sampling (spore trap cassettes)
  • Surface sampling where visible mold is suspected
  • Outdoor baseline sample for comparison
  • Lab analysis by accredited laboratory
  • Full written report with mold types & spore counts
  • Remediation recommendations if mold is found

Who Needs Mold Testing?

Homebuyers in any Arizona property with prior water damage, condensation issues, or visible staining. Anyone experiencing unexplained allergy or respiratory symptoms. Sellers who want to proactively rule out mold before listing.

Why Mold Matters in Arizona

Cladosporium, Aspergillus, and other mold species thrive after monsoon flooding, HVAC leaks, and plumbing failures. Left untreated, mold causes serious health effects and can cost $5,000–$30,000 in professional remediation. Early detection is always the smarter, cheaper path.

Thermal Imaging Inspection in Arizona

Infrared thermal imaging sees what the human eye cannot. Our FLIR infrared cameras detect temperature differentials in walls, ceilings, floors, and around windows - revealing hidden moisture intrusion, energy loss, electrical hot spots, and insulation deficiencies that a standard visual inspection would miss entirely.

In Arizona, thermal imaging is especially valuable for identifying AC duct leaks (a major energy cost), hidden roof leak paths after monsoon season, and electrical components running hot inside panels or walls.

What's Included

  • Full property infrared scan with FLIR thermal camera
  • Electrical panel & wiring hot spot detection
  • Moisture intrusion behind walls & ceilings
  • Roof leak path identification
  • Insulation gaps & energy loss mapping
  • HVAC duct leak detection
  • Annotated thermal image report with findings

Who Needs This?

Any buyer wanting maximum protection. Homeowners concerned about high energy bills. Anyone purchasing a home that has had previous water damage, roof repairs, or electrical work. Can be added to any full inspection or ordered standalone.

Our certified pool inspectors evaluate all mechanical systems, structural components, safety features, and water quality indicators - giving you a comprehensive picture of what you're buying.

What's Included

Sewer Scope Inspection in Arizona

The main sewer line is entirely underground and invisible during a standard home inspection - yet a failed sewer line is one of the most expensive surprises a homeowner can face. Sewer scope inspections in Phoenix, Scottsdale, and across Arizona are one of the best-value services we offer, and among the most frequently recommended add-ons for any property built before 2000.

We run a high-definition waterproof camera through the main sewer line from the cleanout to the municipal connection, recording the entire journey in HD video and flagging any areas of concern.

What's Included

  • HD camera inspection of main sewer line
  • Identification of root intrusions & blockages
  • Pipe condition assessment (cracks, offsets, deterioration)
  • Grease buildup & debris accumulation documentation
  • Pipe material identification (clay, ABS, PVC, cast iron)
  • Depth & location markings for repair planning
  • Full HD video recording delivered with report
  • Clear repair/replacement recommendations

Who Should Get a Sewer Scope?

Any buyer purchasing a home built before 2000. Buyers of homes with large, mature trees in the yard (root intrusion is extremely common). Anyone buying a home that has had slow drains, sewage odors, or past plumbing issues. This inspection is also highly recommended for any home inspection near me search in older Phoenix neighborhoods.

The Real Cost of Skipping This

Sewer line replacement in Arizona averages $4,000–$15,000 depending on depth and length. A trenchless re-lining can run $8,000–$20,000. A $225 sewer scope inspection catches these problems before you own them.
