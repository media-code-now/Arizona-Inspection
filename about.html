Our Story

Built on Honesty. Driven by Expertise.

Arizona Inspection Services was founded in 2013 by home inspection professionals who believed buyers deserved more - more thoroughness, more clarity, and more honesty than the industry standard was delivering.

What started as a single-inspector operation serving the Phoenix metro has grown into one of Arizona's most trusted home inspection companies - with thousands of completed inspections across Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler, Glendale, Tempe, and beyond.

We've built our reputation not on volume, but on quality. Every inspector on our team is AZ state licensed and nationally certified. Every report is thorough, photo-documented, and delivered within 24 hours. Every client gets direct access to their inspector after the report - because a home inspection without support is just a PDF.

We know Arizona homes. We know caliche foundations, flat desert roofs, monsoon moisture issues, 115°F attic conditions, and the unique challenges that come with buying or selling property in the Valley. That local expertise makes a measurable difference in the quality of our inspections - and in the outcomes for our clients.
